Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026 · Draft for review by qualified counsel

This policy explains what ClearPaid (“we”, “the app”) does with information when you use the website and the app. We keep data use to what the features need.

1. Who we are

ClearPaid is a prototype built for the PayPal “Build What’s Next” hackathon. It runs on PayPal’s sandbox. It does not move real money. Controller contact: [name and contact email to be added before public release].

3. PayPal

When you send an invoice, the invoice details go to PayPal’s sandbox API. PayPal processes that data under its own privacy policy. In sandbox mode the data is test data.

4. AI providers

When AI keys are configured, the text of a client message is sent to the first available provider in this order: Groq, Google Gemini, OpenRouter. If one provider fails, the next is tried. If none works, rule-based logic runs on our server and no text leaves it. Each provider has its own privacy policy and may keep requests for a period. Do not paste sensitive data such as bank details, identity numbers or passwords into the AI reply tool.

5. Identity documents

The KYC prototype reads a document image in your browser. It is not uploaded. It is a demonstration only and must not be used to verify real people. Real identity checks are regulated and need specialist providers and proper legal basis.

6. Your choices and rights

You can clear saved invoices from the app or delete your browser’s site data at any time. You can ask us for access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold. If you are in India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 gives you specific rights, and you may complain to the Data Protection Board once it is in operation. Counsel should confirm the final rights list before launch.

7. Children

The service is for business use by adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

We will update this page when features change, and show the new date at the top.
