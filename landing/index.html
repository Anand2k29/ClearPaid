How it works

From client bill to money in your account

Four steps. Anything that touches a client or money waits for your approval.

1

Scan or upload the bill

A photo, PDF or email is read on your device. You check every field before it is used.

2

The invoice is created and emailed to the client. PayPal confirms each payment.

3

Reply, with your approval

Type or speak the client's message. The AI suggests a reply, and nothing is sent until you do it.

Security

Strict by default, because money is involved

PayPal holds card and bank details. We never do. Here is what the build enforces today.

  • Access token

    Every API call needs a bearer token, compared in constant time. It stays in the browser tab only.

  • Locked-down pages

    A strict Content Security Policy, no framing, no MIME sniffing, and no caching of API responses.

  • Rate limits

    Per-minute limits for every caller, plus per-minute and daily caps on AI calls.

  • Signed proofs

    HMAC-SHA256 signatures. Any change to the details breaks the signature.

  • No double sends

    Duplicate invoice numbers and repeat sends are refused. Webhooks must be signed and recent.

  • AI stays on topic

    Off-topic and rule-changing requests are refused before any model runs.

  • Scans stay local

    Bill photos and PDFs are read in your browser and are not uploaded.

  • Quiet errors

    Server and provider errors return a generic message. Keys never reach the browser.

Not finished yet

We will not claim more than the build does. Before real customers we still need user accounts with sign-in, HTTPS on a public domain, removal of the remaining inline scripts, an independent security review, and a written data-retention schedule. See Security & Data.

FAQ

Questions

Does the app send money?

No. Clients pay PayPal. The app creates and sends invoices and shows what PayPal reports. It never moves money between people.

Does AI send messages for me?

No. It suggests a reply, and you copy it or share it yourself. Nothing is sent automatically.

What happens to my bill photos?

Scans are read on your device and not uploaded to our server. Text recognition downloads its language data once from a public CDN.

Can several freelancers share one project?

Yes. Each person gets their own invoice for their share and is paid separately. The app does not hold the money.

Is this tax or legal advice?

No. The money view and checklists are estimates and guides. Ask a chartered accountant about tax, and your bank about export documents.

Can I use it with a real PayPal account now?

Not yet. The current build works on PayPal's sandbox only. A live release needs a security review, sign-in and a data policy first.
